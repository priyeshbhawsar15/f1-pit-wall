# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

The product serves two human sim racers competing in the same match and the people following their race. The racers need a clear comparison of their performance, while spectators need enough context to understand the match without interpreting raw telemetry.

## Product Purpose

During a live session, the dashboard makes the two human players' race position, pace, strategy, current battle, and comparative performance understandable at a glance. Fun statistics help everyone following the match see how each player is performing.

After the race, the history experience helps the players compare their performances, identify where one gained or fell behind, and understand what each could have improved. Success means telemetry becomes a useful and engaging account of the competition rather than a collection of disconnected measurements.

## Positioning

Focused two-player race intelligence that turns live and historical telemetry into a clear, competitive match story.

## Brand Personality

Informative, helpful, and competitive. The product should communicate with modern restraint while keeping the energy and stakes of a head-to-head race visible.

## Anti-references

The dashboard should not feel like an overcrowded engineering console or a generic admin template. It should avoid both excessive density and information-light minimalism, presenting a substantial amount of useful race information without making viewers work to find the story.

## Design Principles

1. **Keep the two players at the center.** Their relative performance and unfolding battle take priority over the rest of the field.
2. **Make the race readable at a glance.** Lead with the comparisons and state changes that explain what is happening now.
3. **Turn telemetry into insight.** Explain gains, losses, turning points, and improvement opportunities instead of stopping at raw measurements.
4. **Use density with purpose.** Show a useful amount of information, but give every metric a clear role in the viewer's understanding.
5. **Make competition engaging, not noisy.** Use statistics and presentation to reinforce the match story without resorting to arcade-like clutter.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No specific formal accessibility target or known user need has been identified.
